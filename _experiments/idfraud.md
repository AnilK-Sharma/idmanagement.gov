This playbook is a collaboration between the Federal Chief Information Security Officer Council Identity, Credential, and Access Management Subcommittee and the Digital Identity Community of Practice. This playbook was developed by GSA’s Office of Technology Policy’s Identity and Trusted Access Division. Key contributions were made by the members of the Defense Advanced Research Project Agency SemaFor Program team.

This playbook serves as a resource and provides a foundational understanding of identity fraud techniques, detection methods, and mitigation strategies. By establishing a common terminology and framework, this guide empowers agencies to:

- Recognize the diverse landscape of identity fraud techniques. 
- Detect suspicious activities and potential threats.
- Implement effective mitigation strategies to protect sensitive data and systems.
- Foster cross-agency collaboration and information sharing to combat identity fraud collectively.
